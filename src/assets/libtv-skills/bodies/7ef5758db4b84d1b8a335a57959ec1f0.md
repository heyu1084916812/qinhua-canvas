# Triple FX Brand Motion

Layer three simultaneous visual effects — crystal shattering/growth, dot-matrix particle assembly, and dual-color neon streams — in the same frame. Start from pure black, let all three layers drive the brand wordmark to emerge from nothing, and lock to a clean final frame.

## Step 1: Identify assets and target

Obtain the brand wordmark image as the visual anchor. Identify: readable text content, letterform structure (stroke directions, cutout slots, negative space), wordmark color tone, layout. Ask only when wordmark content or target duration is genuinely ambiguous.

## Step 2: Analyze letterform structure

Map each stroke's direction, endpoints, corners, and cutout slot positions, plus the geometric negative space of each letter or character. Confirm the final wordmark color tone and background color (default near-black navy #050A14).

## Step 3: Write the three-segment prompt

Compose a full prompt following the three-phase structure below. Duration 15s, 16:9, 1080p, Minimax H3.

**Core rules:**

- Opening frame must be pure black — absolutely no wordmark outline, text shape, or logo form
- All three effects must be layered simultaneously in the same frame — never sequential
- Wordmark must emerge from zero through the combined three-layer effects, locking clean at the end

**Phase 1 (00:00–00:02) Pure black open:** Absolute black, no elements, only faint ambient light, low industrial synth begins.

**Phase 2 (00:02–00:12) Three layers simultaneously:**

Layer 1 — Crystal shatter and growth: Transparent multifaceted crystal sphere falls from above, shatters on impact into sharp polygonal shards flying outward; shards then gather along stroke edges, crystallizing and growing along stroke cutout slots and letter outlines, cold hard side-back lighting producing strong reflections and refractions.

Layer 2 — Particle assembly (simultaneous with Layer 1): Masses of glowing apricot particles converge from all sides toward center, combining with crystal shards in stroke areas, particle trails tracing letterform outlines, gradually sketching the wordmark from zero.

Layer 3 — Dual-color neon streams (simultaneous with Layers 1 & 2): Royal blue and apricot neon light streams weave through the frame, orbiting the assembling wordmark, energy nodes bursting at stroke intersections, streams interweaving with particles and crystal shards in the same space.

All three layers visible simultaneously, cross-layered, jointly driving the wordmark to emerge from nothing.

**Phase 3 (00:12–00:15) Dissolve and lock:** All crystals, particles, and streams dissolve simultaneously; wordmark locks clean at center, wide margins, clean tail frame, no extra elements.

## Step 4: Confirmation card

Show prompt summary and confirm: wordmark text, color tone, background color, duration (default 15s), aspect ratio (default 16:9), all three layers retained (default yes).

## Step 5: Generate and verify

After confirmation, generate the video. Verify: pure black open with no wordmark, three effects simultaneous (not sequential), wordmark emerges from zero (not pre-displayed), clean tail frame. Retry only with a materially restructured prompt while keeping wordmark facts fixed.

## Boundaries

Best for tech brand intros and high-impact logo reveals. Not for pre-displaying the wordmark, single-effect animations, sequential playback, long campaigns, or general editing. Hard prohibitions: opening wordmark silhouette, sequential three-layer playback, people, extra text, platform watermarks.
