# Video Frame Poster Design

## Core Methodology

### Frame Selection Logic — Visual Narrative Extraction

Video-to-poster design requires identifying **cinematic peak moments** that encapsulate story essence:

- **Action climax frames**: Moments of maximum visual tension (bow fully drawn, arrow mid-flight, sword at apex)
- **Character expression peaks**: Decisive facial expressions or body language that convey narrative themes
- **Compositional balance**: Frames with inherent visual weight distribution suitable for text overlay
- **Cultural iconography**: Elements carrying symbolic meaning (armor, weapons, architecture) that reinforce thematic content

**Selection criteria hierarchy**:

1. Thematic alignment with title/subtitle (70% weight)
2. Compositional suitability for text placement (20% weight)
3. Visual clarity and detail retention (10% weight)

### Chinese Minimalist Poster Framework —留白美学 (Liúbái Aesthetics)

Traditional Chinese visual design operates on **controlled emptiness** as primary compositional tool:

**Spatial allocation model**:

- **主体区 (Subject zone)**: 30-40% of canvas — hero image/character positioned in lower-third or right-third
- **留白区 (Void zone)**: 50-60% of canvas — empty or subtly textured space (ink wash, mist, gradient)
- **文字区 (Text zone)**: 10-20% of canvas — calligraphic title integrated into void zone

**Visual hierarchy**:

1. **主标题 (Primary title)**: Large golden/vermillion seal script or clerical script characters, vertical or horizontal arrangement
2. **副标题 (Subtitle)**: Smaller complementary text in regular script, positioned to balance primary title
3. **视觉焦点 (Visual anchor)**: Character/object extracted from video frame, maintaining dramatic lighting and texture

**Color palette constraints**:

- Base layer: Ink wash grayscale (black to warm gray)
- Accent colors: Imperial gold, cinnabar red, jade green (maximum 2 accent colors)
- Saturation cap: 60% for accents, 20% for backgrounds

### Text-Image Integration Logic

Text placement must follow **calligraphic breathing space** principles:

- **Vertical text flow**: Right-to-left reading order for classical titles, positioned in upper void zone or flanking subject
- **Horizontal text flow**: Centered or upper-third placement, allowing 1.5x character height clearance above subject
- **Stroke weight harmony**: Title stroke weight should visually balance with subject's darkest elements (armor edges, weapon silhouettes)
- **Contrast anchoring**: Text color must maintain 4:1 contrast ratio against background, 3:1 against subject edges

## Workflow

### 1. Preparation Stage — Frame Analysis

**Input inventory**:

- Video source with clear narrative arc
- Title text (primary message, 2-6 Chinese characters optimal)
- Subtitle text (secondary context, optional)
- Target aspect ratio (vertical 3:4 for social media, horizontal 16:9 for display)

**Frame extraction checklist**:

- Identify 3-5 candidate frames representing narrative peaks
- Verify frame resolution sufficiency (minimum 1080p equivalent)
- Check for motion blur or compression artifacts
- Assess compositional balance (subject position, background complexity)

**Thematic alignment decision**:

- Map title semantic content to visual metaphors in candidate frames
- Example: "封狼居胥" (imperial conquest glory) → frames showing martial prowess, decisive action, heroic posture
- Prioritize frames where character action physically embodies title meaning

### 2. Initial Design Stage — Compositional Construction

**Layout framework execution**:

1. **Subject extraction and positioning**:

   - Isolate character/object from selected frame
   - Remove distracting background elements while preserving atmospheric context (distant mountains, mist, architecture)
   - Position subject in lower-third (vertical) or right-third (horizontal) to maximize void space

2. **Void zone creation**:

   - Generate ink wash texture gradient in empty space using watercolor blending
   - Integrate subtle environmental elements (clouds, mountain silhouettes) at 10-20% opacity
   - Maintain clear visual separation between subject and void (avoid edge bleeding)

3. **Text placement architecture**:

   - **Vertical posters**: Title in upper void, subtitle vertically aligned to subject's left edge
   - **Horizontal posters**: Title in left void zone, subtitle below or integrated into background texture
   - Apply golden ratio grid (1:1.618) for text-subject distance calculation

**Typography execution**:

- Primary title: 18-24% of canvas height, seal script or bold clerical style
- Subtitle: 6-10% of canvas height, regular script with increased character spacing
- Color: Metallic gold (#C9A961) or cinnabar red (#8B2500) with 20% luminosity gradient

### 3. Iteration Stage — Refinement Dimensions

**Dimension 1: Compositional balance audit**

- Visual weight distribution: Does subject + text create stable triangle composition?
- Breathing space check: Is void zone at least 50% of total canvas?
- Edge tension: Are key elements positioned away from canvas edges (minimum 8% margin)?

**Dimension 2: Cultural authenticity verification**

- Calligraphic stroke integrity: Do characters maintain brush stroke directionality?
- Color harmony: Does palette align with traditional Chinese painting (靑绿山水 or 水墨风格)?
- Symbolic coherence: Do visual elements reinforce thematic narrative?

**Dimension 3: Technical quality standards**

- Text legibility: 4:1 contrast ratio against all backgrounds
- Subject detail retention: Armor texture, fabric folds, weapon details remain crisp
- Resolution verification: Final output meets minimum 1920px on long edge for horizontal, 1080px for vertical

**Acceptance criteria**:

- Title immediately readable at thumbnail scale (200px width)
- Subject action clearly conveys narrative theme without context
- Void space creates visual breathing room (not emptiness requiring fill)
- Cultural aesthetic cohesion (no anachronistic or Western design elements)

## Key Constraints & Standards

### Immutable Rules

- **Language specificity**: All text must be in Chinese characters with appropriate calligraphic styling (no Latin fonts unless bilingual requirement specified)
- **Aspect ratio adherence**: Vertical (3:4, 9:16) or horizontal (16:9, 2:1) must be determined upfront and maintained throughout
- **Minimum void space**: 50% of canvas must remain visually empty or subtly textured

### Fidelity Rules

- **Frame authenticity**: Extracted frames must preserve original lighting, color temperature, and character proportions from video source
- **Cultural iconography accuracy**: Armor styles, weapon types, architectural elements must match historical period implied by title
- **Calligraphic stroke direction**: Character strokes must follow traditional brush writing order (横竖撇捺 sequence)

### Consistency Rules

- **Text-subject relationship**: Title semantic meaning must align with subject's action/expression (no abstract titles on literal imagery)
- **Color temperature unity**: Warm-toned subjects require warm text colors (gold, red); cool-toned subjects allow cool accents (jade, silver)
- **Compositional axis**: All elements (subject, title, subtitle) should align to single vertical or horizontal axis for visual coherence

### Quality Thresholds

- **Text legibility minimum**: 4:1 contrast ratio against immediate background, verified at 25% zoom
- **Subject detail retention**: Fine details (armor rivets, fabric weave) must remain visible at 100% zoom
- **Edge clearance standard**: No critical elements within 8% of canvas edges (prevents cropping issues across platforms)

## Advanced Techniques

### Multi-Layered Depth Construction

For premium visual impact, implement **three-plane depth model**:

- **Foreground plane** (100% opacity): Subject character with sharp focus and preserved details
- **Midground plane** (40-60% opacity): Environmental elements (mist, falling leaves, dust particles) adding atmospheric context
- **Background plane** (10-20% opacity): Ink wash texture, distant mountains, or abstract cultural patterns providing tonal foundation

Apply Gaussian blur gradient: foreground (0px) → midground (8-15px) → background (20-40px)

### Dynamic Text Integration

Move beyond static text placement with **kinetic typography principles**:

- **Directional alignment**: Align text baseline parallel to subject's action vector (arrow trajectory, sword swing arc)
- **Stroke echo**: Duplicate title at 15% opacity, offset by 3-5px in direction opposite to subject's movement, creating motion blur effect
- **Character stagger**: In vertical text arrangements, offset alternating characters by 5-10px horizontally to break rigidity while maintaining readability

### Cultural Symbolism Layering

Elevate thematic depth through **embedded iconography**:

- Integrate subtle traditional patterns (云纹, 龙纹, 回字纹) at 5-8% opacity in void zones
- Position according to symbolic meaning: dragons in upper zones (heaven), water patterns in lower zones (earth)
- Maintain 70%+ void space even with pattern integration to preserve minimalist aesthetic
