## When to Use

Use this skill when the user asks to:

- generate a realistic human portrait prompt
- create a fictional human character
- generate a person from age and gender
- customize facial features or temperament
- create documentary-style portraits
- create cinematic portraits
- create studio portraits
- create realistic elderly, adult, or young characters
- produce high-detail photorealistic AI portrait prompts

Typical requests:

- “生成一个70岁亚洲男性老人”
- “25岁女性，清冷感，高颧骨”
- “40岁男性，成熟商务感”
- “80岁老人，纪实摄影”
- “生成一个真实感很强的人像提示词”

Do not require a reference image.

---

## Required Input

At minimum, identify:

- Age
- Gender

If the user provides only age and gender, automatically complete all remaining characteristics.

## Age Consistency Rules

The face must visually match the requested age.

Never generate:

- elderly people with young skin
- young adults with excessive aging
- a generic young face with wrinkles simply added on top
- unnaturally flawless skin at any age

Use age-appropriate changes in:

- facial fat distribution
- skin elasticity
- wrinkles
- eye bags
- nasolabial folds
- jawline
- cheek volume
- hair density
- hair color
- pigmentation
- facial sagging

### 18–25

Use:

- fuller facial volume
- relatively smooth skin
- visible but fine pores
- subtle natural imperfections
- minimal eye wrinkles
- fresh but not plastic skin

### 26–35

Use:

- mature facial structure
- visible pores
- subtle eye lines
- mild nasolabial folds
- clearer jaw and cheek contours
- realistic skin texture

### 36–50

Use:

- light forehead lines
- crow's feet
- visible nasolabial folds
- mild eye bags or dark circles
- reduced skin elasticity
- slightly reduced facial fat

### 51–65

Use:

- obvious forehead wrinkles
- visible eye wrinkles
- stronger nasolabial folds
- eye bags
- mild facial sagging
- stronger skin texture
- partial gray hair where appropriate

### 66–80

Use:

- deep wrinkles
- visible eye bags
- deeper eye sockets
- reduced cheek volume
- softer jawline
- stronger nasolabial folds
- gray or white hair
- realistic elderly skin texture

### 80+

Use:

- deep layered wrinkles
- obvious skin laxity
- pigmentation or age spots
- hollow cheeks
- deeper eye sockets
- fine gray or white hair
- strong but believable signs of aging

Do not make older people look like young models with added wrinkle decals.

## Facial Design Rules

Do not create generic faces.

Create a specific facial combination using:

- face shape
- forehead proportion
- eyebrow thickness and direction
- eye size and shape
- eyelid type
- eye depth
- cheekbone prominence
- nose bridge
- nose width
- nose tip
- lip thickness
- jaw shape
- chin shape

If the user only provides age and gender, introduce natural variation so repeated generations do not produce the same facial archetype.

Useful facial types include:

- oval face
- round face
- square face
- long face
- narrow face
- high cheekbones
- broad jaw
- sharp jawline
- soft jawline
- deep-set eyes
- monolid eyes
- narrow eyes
- rounded eyes
- high nose bridge
- broad nose
- thin lips
- fuller lips

Combine traits coherently.

## Skin Realism Rules

Realistic skin is mandatory.

Use when appropriate:

- visible pores
- subtle uneven skin tone
- natural oil highlights
- fine facial hair
- minor blemishes
- small skin imperfections
- realistic wrinkles
- subtle redness
- under-eye texture
- lip texture
- realistic skin translucency

Young characters must still have pores and natural texture.

Avoid:

- plastic skin
- wax-like skin
- excessive beauty retouching
- completely flawless skin
- fake pore overlays
- over-sharpened texture
- airbrushed skin

- strand direction
- volume

Prioritize:

- realistic hair texture

- unnaturally perfect edges

## Clothing Rules

If clothing is unspecified, use simple clothing that does not compete with the face.

Examples:

- black crew-neck T-shirt
- dark knitwear
- white shirt
- neutral sweater
- simple cotton top
- understated jacket
- dark turtleneck

Default:

- no obvious logo
- no excessive jewelry
- no elaborate patterns
- no visually distracting accessories

Older characters should generally use simpler and more age-appropriate clothing unless the user specifies otherwise.

---

## Pose Rules

If no pose is specified, use a stable portrait pose.

Default:

- head-and-shoulders portrait
- shoulders relaxed
- body facing camera or turned 20–30 degrees
- head gently turned toward camera
- chin slightly lowered
- natural neck posture
- restrained body language

Use simple poses unless the user explicitly requests an action.

The face remains the main visual subject.

---

## Composition Rules

Default framing:

- close-up portrait
- head-and-shoulders
- chest-up portrait
- centered composition or subtle rule of thirds
- eyes positioned near the upper third

Supported framing:

- extreme close-up
- close-up
- bust portrait
- half-body portrait
- environmental portrait

Do not crop important facial features awkwardly.

---

## Camera Rules

Default:

- 85mm portrait lens
- f/1.8 to f/2.8
- shallow depth of field
- eye-level camera position
- realistic facial perspective

Use:

- 35mm for environmental portraits
- 50mm for natural documentary framing
- 85mm for classic portrait photography
- 105mm or 135mm only when stronger compression is appropriate

Do not overload the prompt with unnecessary camera specifications.

---

## Lighting Rules

Choose lighting according to style.

### Natural Portrait

Use:

- large soft key light

- soft facial shadow transitions
- subtle fill light
- natural catchlights

### Documentary Portrait

Use:

- realistic directional light
- stronger texture visibility
- restrained contrast
- minimal beautification
- believable environmental light

### Cinematic Portrait

Use:

- Rembrandt lighting
- side lighting
- low-key lighting
- subtle rim light
- controlled contrast
- cinematic tonal separation

The face must remain readable.

Avoid:

- crushed facial shadows
- clipped highlights
- unrealistic colored light unless requested

## Common Mistakes

### Generic Face

Problem: The prompt says only “handsome man” or “beautiful woman”.

Fix: Specify face shape, eyes, nose, lips, cheekbones, jawline, skin condition, and age-specific details.

### Fake Aging

Problem: An elderly person has youthful facial volume and smooth skin.

Fix: Adjust facial fat, eye sockets, wrinkles, hair density, skin elasticity, and pigmentation together.

### Plastic Skin

Problem: The face is too clean and airbrushed.

Fix: Add pores, subtle uneven tone, fine hair, minor imperfections, realistic highlights, and age-appropriate texture.

### Too Many Style Words

Problem: The prompt is filled with “masterpiece, best quality, 16K” but the person remains vague.

Fix: Spend more prompt detail on the human being.

### Conflicting Photography Language

Problem: The prompt mixes incompatible lenses, lighting setups, or styles.

Fix: Choose one coherent photographic setup.

## Final Rule

**Character specificity &gt; age realism &gt; skin realism &gt; photography style &gt; resolution buzzwords.**

Always make the human being specific before adding visual style.
